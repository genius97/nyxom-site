Why “Nyxom”?

Our name comes from “Nyx” — the goddess of night in ancient Greek mythology. It is at night that the sky clears and the stars appear: the same stars Omani navigators used for centuries to cross seas and deserts.

We do the same with the tools of our age: we take your organisation's scattered data — like scattered stars — and connect it into intelligent maps your decisions can navigate by. That is the meaning of our logo: a letter drawn in stars.

✧ Our mission

To put the power of AI in the hands of Omani and Gulf organisations — public and private — through solutions built from scratch to understand our language, culture and way of doing business, not translations of experiences made for someone else.

✦ Our vision

To be the first name that comes to mind when any organisation in the region thinks of AI — and to contribute, from Oman, to a digital economy led by the region's minds rather than its importers, in service of Oman Vision 2040.
