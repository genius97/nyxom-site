Custom AI applications

We design and build complete AI applications — not lab experiments or demos, but products ready for real operation. We specialise in solutions that understand Arabic and its Gulf dialects, respect your data privacy, and integrate with the systems you already run.

  • Conversational assistants & AI agents fluent in Arabic dialects
  • Multi-agent systems for complex business tasks
  • Document, image and voice understanding
  • Content & enterprise-knowledge engines
  • Full model flexibility — cloud or self-hosted
  • Native RTL Arabic dashboards

✦ For whom? Government bodies and companies that want a real AI product speaking their customers' language.

Intelligent automation

How many hours does your team spend on repetitive tasks a smart system could handle? We automate customer service, internal operations and workflows — with systems that run 24/7 and plug into the channels your customers actually use, WhatsApp first.

  • Smart auto-replies that understand dialect, voice and images
  • Automated bookings, orders and follow-ups
  • Back-office and billing automation
  • WhatsApp, email & local payment-gateway integration
  • Real-time automation dashboards
