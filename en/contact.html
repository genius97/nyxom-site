FAQ

Before you ask — answered

How do we start working together?

Message us on WhatsApp or email with a short description of your idea or challenge. We'll schedule a free discovery session to understand your goal, then come back with a clear picture of the solution, scope and cost — you decide after that, with zero obligation.

Do you work with government entities?

Yes. We design our solutions to meet public-sector requirements in Oman and the Gulf — from data governance and privacy to hosting options and deployment inside your own environment — and we serve private companies of every size.

How long does a solution take?

It depends on scope, but our method relies on short cycles: you see a working prototype within a few weeks, not months — and we commit to a clear timeline from the moment we agree.

Do you provide support after launch?

Always. We offer care packages covering monitoring, support and continuous improvement — smart solutions get better with use, and we stay to make sure yours does.

Do we own the solution after delivery?

Yes — you receive the code, the documentation and everything needed to be a true owner of what we built, with full freedom to choose who operates and evolves it in the future.
